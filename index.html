Flight Tools for British Airways Crew.

A focussed suite of practical tools designed for Pre-Flight Briefings, easier AOR handling, FDP calculations and adding roster information to Apple iCloud calendars.

General Feedback

Aircraft Info

Lookup aircraft details quickly using the aircraft registration. Information provided includes WiFi availability and location of spare crew jump seats.

Briefing & AORs

Access aircraft AORs with a single click, or Delivery Standards information more easily. Input aircraft registration details for more detailed information.

FDP Calculator

Calculate FDP limits, latest chocks on times as well as delay scenarios. Users can then easily email this information to their on-the-day Flight Crew team. SCCMs can also create a pre-flight briefing presentation to use in briefing rooms with personalized information.

iCal

Upload a PDF of of your roster and sync it with your Apple iCloud calendar.
